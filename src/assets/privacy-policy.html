FloSmith Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

FloSmith ("the app") is a class planning tool for Pilates instructors. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Data We Collect

FloSmith stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Exercise timing samples (duration per exercise, used to calculate averages)
  • Class flows and programs you build within the app
  • Client notes you enter manually
  • App preferences such as language and timer settings
  • An optional instructor email address you may choose to save

All data is stored using your device's local storage. None of this data is transmitted to any server, third party, or cloud service.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, location, or contact information
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not have access to any data stored on your device

3. Client Data

If you enter notes about your Pilates clients, that information is stored solely on your device and is never transmitted. You are responsible for handling client information in accordance with any applicable privacy laws in your jurisdiction.

4. Children's Privacy

FloSmith is intended for use by adult fitness professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13.

5. Data Deletion

All app data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app from your device. There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: support@flosmith.app
