FloSmith Support

FloSmith is a class planning and timing tool for Pilates instructors. If you need help, you'll find answers below — or reach out directly.

Contact

Email us at support@flosmith.app and we'll get back to you within 1–2 business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I build a class flow?

Go to the Library tab, browse or search for exercises, and tap Add to Flow. Then open the Plan tab to view and reorder your flow before class.

How does the exercise timer work?

Start the timer on the Timer page. The app tracks how long you spend on each exercise and builds a rolling average over time. Tap Next Exercise when you're ready to advance — the timer never advances automatically.

Will my data be lost if I delete the app?

Yes. All data (flows, timing history, client notes, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently. There is no cloud backup at this time.

How do I change the app language?

Go to the Profile tab and select your preferred language from the language picker. The app supports English, Arabic, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese.

Can I reuse class structures?

Yes. Save any flow as a Program from the Plan tab. Saved programs appear in the Programs section and can be loaded and customized any time.

How do I track notes for individual clients?

Open the Clients section from the Profile tab. Add a client and enter notes about injuries, preferences, or goals. These notes are stored locally on your device.

Something isn't working — what should I do?

Try force-closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, email us at support@flosmith.app with a description of what happened and your iOS version.

Privacy

FloSmith does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information stays on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
